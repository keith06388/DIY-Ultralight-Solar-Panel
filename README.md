# DIY-Ultralight-Solar-Panel
10W backpacking solar panel with built in battery charger and usb-c charging port

* Solder (low temp for magnet connections and soldering to the cells): https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256809057430922.html
* Solar cells: https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256810524371776.html
* Wire (internal wire for panel): 22awg silicone insulated flexible
* Adhesive to hold down cells: Double sided acrylic PSA
* Laminator pouches: 5mil, preferably "dry erase compatible" since they are slightly harder top sheet
* Panel magnets (for holding folded panel closed): https://www.kjmagnetics.com/b631-n52-neodymium-block-magnet

Notes on slicing the STL's.  Take a look at my projects I uploaded from Orca slicer for my Elegoo CC.  I pulled in both the screen and frame files together and I sliced the screen with gyroid infill with no top, bottom, or sides.  I used 11% infill and I doubled the line width on the top and bottom layer of the screen to add stiffness (like an ibeam).
